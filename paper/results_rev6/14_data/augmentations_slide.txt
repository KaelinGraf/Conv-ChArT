SYNTHETIC DATA -- ONE SLIDE
Six bullets condensed from the full 24-item list in augmentations.txt.
Every frame is generated on the fly; there is no fixed training set.


    Real photographic backgrounds
        COCO images, with the board histogram-matched and relit to the local
        background luminance so it belongs in the scene rather than being pasted
        on top of it.

    Full board placement
        Scale 16 to 128 px between corners, any in-plane rotation, out-of-plane
        tilt to 60 deg, partial views off the frame edge.

    Occlusion by real objects
        Segmented object cutouts (SAM2) and rectangular occluders. A hidden
        corner is relabelled not-visible, so the network is taught to abstain
        rather than to hallucinate.

    Optical degradation
        Motion blur, defocus, glare and specular highlights, water droplets on
        the lens, vignetting.

    Sensor model
        Photon shot noise and read noise (Poissonian-Gaussian), fixed-pattern
        noise and PRNU, brightness down to near-black.

    The deployment domain
        Lit-minus-unlit differencing pairs: two exposures with a daylight
        pedestal and an LED illumination lobe, subtracted. This is what the
        robot actually sees.


SEVERITIES ARE CENTRED ON THE REAL CAMERA -- an OV2311-class industrial sensor:
global shutter, monochrome, 940 nm bandpass, no artistic ISP. The benchmark is
meant to be hard where the deployment is hard, not hard everywhere.
